# Output Type: Book Proposal

A manuscript proposal for Israeli publishers (or Hebrew-language division of international publishers). The document a publisher's acquisitions editor reads to decide whether to read your full manuscript.

## When this output type is right

- **Submitting non-fiction book** to Kinneret Zmora-Bitan, Yedioth Books, Pardes, Babel
- **Bilingual submission** — Hebrew proposal for Israeli publisher, English proposal for parallel international submission
- **Re-submission after rejection** — refined proposal with positioning shift
- **Co-author collaboration proposal** — multiple authors, shared book
- **Translation pitch** — bringing an English book to Hebrew market

## Structural template (typical 15–25 pages)

### Cover page
- Title (Hebrew + English subtitle if bilingual aspiration)
- Author name + brief credential line
- Word count target
- Submission date
- Comparable titles (1 line)

### Executive summary (1–2 pages)
- The book in 2–3 sentences
- The market and why now
- Author qualifications in 1 sentence
- Expected length and timeline

### Detailed synopsis (3–5 pages)
- **Opening hook** — the moment / story that frames the book
- **Core thesis** — what this book argues / teaches / explores
- **Chapter-by-chapter map** — one paragraph per chapter, 8–15 chapters typical
- **Voice and approach** — how it's written (memoir / how-to / analytical / narrative non-fiction)

### Market analysis (2–3 pages)
- **Target reader** — demographics + psychographics + Hebrew-reading subset
- **Market size** — Israeli book market segment, comparable title sales, audiobook potential
- **Comparable titles** — 5–8 books, with what each does well + what your book does differently
- **Competitive advantage** — why this book vs the alternatives

### Author bio (1 page)
- Professional bio with Hebrew + English variants
- Platform — social media follower count, podcast, speaking engagements
- Previous publications
- Why YOU are the right author for THIS book

### Sample chapters (2–3 chapters, full polished prose)
- Chapter 1 in full — typically the strongest chapter
- One body chapter — proving you can sustain the voice
- Chapter outline for remaining chapters with 200-word summaries

### Marketing & publicity plan (1–2 pages)
- **Author platform leverage** — your audience size and engagement
- **Israeli media targets** — TheMarker, Calcalist, Haaretz Weekend, Galei Tzahal Reading Hour, podcasts
- **Translation potential** — Frankfurt Book Fair pitch, English-market expansion
- **Speaking calendar** — conferences where you can promote
- **Endorsements pipeline** — who would blurb the book

### Timeline (½ page)
- Manuscript completion date
- Delivery dates for editorial passes
- Promotional availability

## Hebrew-specific conventions

- **Submit in Hebrew** to Israeli publishers (with English summary if pitching international rights)
- **Date in Israeli format** (DD/MM/YYYY)
- **Author bio in third person** for cover page; first-person voice acceptable in sample chapters
- **Use formal address** to acquisitions editor (לכבוד / שלום רב / בכבוד רב)
- **Comparable titles** named with publisher and year (טכנולוגיה ישראלית, ידיעות, 2021)

## Persona pairings

| Book type | Best persona for proposal voice |
|---|---|
| Tech-business / startup memoir | **יואל** (founder confidence) |
| Industry retrospective / analytical | **איתמר** (veteran authority) |
| Literary / leadership | **שירה** (dignified positioning) |
| Personal memoir / intimate non-fiction | **נועה** (vulnerable + authentic) |
| Comic / satirical | **גלעד** (with sample chapter delivering the humor) |

## Sample executive summary (שירה voice, tech-business book)

> ## ספר: *הצומת השקטה* — כיצד שלוש החלטות עיצבו את התעשייה הישראלית בשנים 2014–2024
>
> במהלך עשור אחד, התעשייה הטכנולוגית בישראל גדלה פי שלושה. עברה ממאתיים-וחמישים סטארטאפים פעילים לאלף-שמונה-מאות. ארבעה מהם הפכו לחברות שמעריכים אותן ביותר ממאה מיליארד דולר.
>
> *הצומת השקטה* בוחן את התקופה הזאת לא מנקודת המבט של החלטות מובנות-מאליהן — אלא מנקודת המבט של ההחלטות שכמעט לא נשאלו. עשרה פרקים. שלוש החלטות פר פרק. שלושים מומנטים שעיצבו את התעשייה.
>
> הספר מתבסס על 87 ראיונות עם מייסדים, משקיעים, מהנדסים, ועיתונאים שכיסו את התקופה. הוא מיועד לשני קהלים: לקוראים בתוך התעשייה שרוצים להבין את ההיסטוריה שיצרו, ולקוראים מחוץ לה שמבקשים מסגרת לחשוב על השנים הבאות.
>
> אורך מתוכנן: כ-80,000 מילים. זמן השלמה: 14 חודשים מתחילת העבודה. זמינות לקידום: גבוהה (כותרת פעילה בלינקדאין עם 23,000 עוקבים; דובר קבוע בכנסים בארץ ובחו"ל).

## Validation gates

- [ ] All 8 sections present and proportioned
- [ ] Synopsis tells the entire book — no surprises hidden for the manuscript
- [ ] Comparable titles include 5–8 real, current titles with publisher + year
- [ ] Author bio has both 2-sentence and 1-page versions
- [ ] Sample chapters are polished (not drafts) — they ARE the audition
- [ ] Marketing plan is specific (named outlets, not just "social media")
- [ ] Persona voice held in sample chapters
- [ ] Hebrew grammar clean (formal register; smikhut precise; numbers digital)
- [ ] Total length 15–25 pages (depending on publisher's submission guidelines)

## Israeli publisher submission specifics

| Publisher | Submission format | Response time |
|---|---|---|
| Kinneret Zmora-Bitan Dvir | Email to acquisitions editor; PDF; bio | 6–12 weeks |
| Yedioth Books | Web form + PDF; sample chapters | 8–16 weeks |
| Pardes (academic) | Full proposal + 1 sample chapter + CV | 3–6 months (peer review) |
| Babel | Email; brief proposal; translation rights focus | 4–8 weeks |
