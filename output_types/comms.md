# Output Type: Communications (Press Release / Email / Memo)

Three combined sub-formats for outbound communications.

## Sub-format 1: Press Release

### When to use
- New product launch
- Funding announcement
- M&A announcement
- Executive hire / departure
- Award win
- Regulatory filing

### Length
300–700 words.

### Structure
- **Dateline:** "תל אביב, 22 במאי 2026" / "Tel Aviv, May 22, 2026"
- **Headline:** active voice, 8–14 words
- **Sub-headline:** one sentence context
- **Lede paragraph:** all 5 W's
- **Body:** 2–4 paragraphs with details + quote
- **Quote:** from executive (named, titled)
- **Boilerplate:** "About [Company]" section, identical across releases
- **Press contact:** name, phone, email

### Conventions
- Released to Israeli wire services (Israel Wire, Globes wire) + key journalists
- Often bilingual (Hebrew + English) for Israeli companies with international audience
- Embargoed releases mark "EMBARGOED UNTIL [time]"

### Persona
**דנה** (sharp positioning) or **שירה** (formal authority)

---

## Sub-format 2: Email — Formal

### When to use
- Email to investor
- Email to customer (B2B account, especially enterprise)
- Email to regulator
- Email to government official
- Email applying for job at C-level

### Length
150–500 words.

### Structure
- **Subject line:** clear + specific (not "Quick question")
- **Greeting:** "שלום [name]" / "Dear [name]," — formal
- **Opening:** 1 sentence — purpose
- **Context:** 1–2 paragraphs — background + key info
- **Request / proposal:** explicit ask
- **Close:** "בכבוד רב" / "תודה רבה" with name + title + contact

### Conventions
- Formal Hebrew register
- No filler / no slang
- Numbers digital
- Concise — Israeli executive culture values brevity
- Bilingual common (Hebrew greeting + English body when audience is international)

### Persona
**שירה** (formal-classical) or **דנה** (sharp business)

---

## Sub-format 3: Internal Memo

### When to use
- All-hands announcement
- Policy change communication
- Leadership decision communication
- Team update beyond Slack

### Length
200–800 words.

### Structure
- **To:** recipients (team / division / company)
- **From:** sender + title
- **Date:** Israeli format
- **Subject:** clear
- **Body:** 2–4 paragraphs covering what + why + when + who
- **Action items** (if any): bulleted
- **Q&A invitation:** "questions to [contact]"

### Conventions
- Tone matches company culture (formal or semi-formal)
- Internal-only language acceptable (less PR-polished than press release)
- Hebrew or bilingual depending on company

### Persona
**יואל** (founder voice) or **דנה** (decisive) or **נועה** (vulnerable / honest)

---

## Combined validation gates

- [ ] Format-appropriate length
- [ ] Subject line / headline clear and specific
- [ ] Single purpose per message
- [ ] Persona voice consistent
- [ ] Hebrew grammar clean
- [ ] Date / numbers / currency format correct
- [ ] No filler / no slang in formal sub-formats
- [ ] Contact info or ask explicit
