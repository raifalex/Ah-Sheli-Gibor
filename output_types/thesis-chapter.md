# Output Type: Thesis Chapter

Graduate thesis chapter — MA or PhD. Israeli universities (Hebrew U, Technion, Bar-Ilan, TAU, Weizmann, BGU) with specific institutional conventions.

## When this output type is right

- **MA thesis chapter** (75–150 pages total thesis)
- **PhD dissertation chapter** (150–300 pages total)
- **Sandwich PhD chapter** (built around published papers)
- **Thesis introduction or conclusion chapter**

## Length

Per chapter: 20–60 pages typical. Total thesis bounded by institution (Bar-Ilan: 150 pages max in English department).

## Structural template

### Chapter introduction (~2–4 pages)
- Chapter purpose + relation to dissertation thesis
- Chapter-specific research question
- Chapter outline

### Background (~5–15 pages)
- Relevant prior work for this chapter
- Theoretical framing
- Definitions specific to this chapter

### Methods / approach (~5–15 pages)
- Detailed methodology
- Mathematical / experimental detail
- Reproducibility

### Results (~5–15 pages)
- Findings with tables / figures
- Statistical analysis
- Comparison to literature

### Discussion (~3–8 pages)
- Interpretation
- Limitations
- Connection to other chapters

### Chapter conclusion (~1–2 pages)
- Summary of findings
- Bridge to next chapter

## Israeli institution-specific conventions

| Institution | Conventions |
|---|---|
| **Hebrew U** | Dissertation evaluation by end of 5th year; full thesis in Hebrew acceptable |
| **Technion** | Engineering convention; bilingual common; IEEE-style citations |
| **Bar-Ilan** | 12-pt David / Frank Ruehl Hebrew; Times New Roman / Arial English; 1.5 line spacing; A4 |
| **TAU** | Thesis in Hebrew (doctoral); bilingual title; 5–7 page synopsis |
| **Weizmann** | Sciences; English thesis with Hebrew abstract |
| **BGU** | Mixed; varies by department |

## Hebrew-specific conventions

- **Bilingual title + abstract** standard
- **Hebrew text + English math notation**
- **Formal-academic register** throughout
- **Citation style consistent** (per institution + department)
- **Smikhut precise**
- **First-person voice**: passive Hebrew typical for sciences; active acceptable for humanities

## Persona pairings

- **איתמר** (sciences thesis — patient, structured)
- **שירה** (humanities thesis — literary precision)

## Validation gates

- [ ] Chapter connects to thesis as a whole
- [ ] Bilingual title/abstract per institution
- [ ] Methods reproducible
- [ ] Results rigorous + statistically analyzed
- [ ] Limitations honest
- [ ] Citations consistent
- [ ] Hebrew + English formatting per institution
