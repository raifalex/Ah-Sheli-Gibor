# Output Type: Business Plan

Full business plan — for new business formation, investor introduction (separate from pitch deck), bank loans, government grants (Israeli Innovation Authority).

## When this output type is right

- **Startup business plan** — for investors, banks, or grant applications
- **New product line business plan** — internal proposal within existing company
- **Israeli Innovation Authority grant** — Yozma / KamaTech / KMD grant applications
- **Bank loan application** — to Israeli bank for working capital / equipment
- **Franchise / expansion plan**

## Length

15–40 pages typical. Investor-focused: 20–30. Bank: 15–25. IIA grant: 25–40 (templated).

## Structural template

### Executive summary (~1–2 pages)
- The business in 2–3 paragraphs
- Funding ask + use of funds
- Founder team + qualifications
- 3-year financial projection summary

### Company overview (~1–2 pages)
- Legal structure, founding date, location
- Mission, vision, values
- Current status (revenue, customers, employees)

### Market analysis (~3–5 pages)
- Industry overview
- Target market (TAM/SAM/SOM)
- Customer segments
- Competitive landscape

### Product / service (~2–4 pages)
- What you sell + how it works
- IP, patents, defensibility
- Roadmap

### Marketing & sales strategy (~2–4 pages)
- Customer acquisition channels
- Pricing
- Sales process
- GTM strategy

### Operations (~1–3 pages)
- Production / delivery
- Suppliers / partners
- Quality controls

### Management team (~1–2 pages)
- Founders + key hires
- Bios + relevant experience
- Advisory board

### Financial projections (~3–6 pages)
- Revenue model + assumptions
- 3–5 year P&L
- Cash flow
- Balance sheet (if relevant)
- Break-even analysis
- Sensitivity analysis

### Funding ask (~1 page)
- Amount + use of funds
- Equity / debt / grant mix
- Expected ROI for investors

### Appendices
- Detailed financials
- Customer references
- Technical detail
- Patents / IP

## Hebrew-specific conventions

- **Numbers digital** with currency symbols (₪ / $ / €)
- **Israeli date format**
- **Government bodies** in Hebrew + English first occurrence (רשות החדשנות / Israeli Innovation Authority)
- **Hebrew tax / accounting terms** precise (מע"מ / חשבונית מס / מס הכנסה)
- **Smikhut for compound business terms**
- **Formal-business register**

## Persona pairings

| Plan type | Best persona |
|---|---|
| Investor-facing | **יואל** (founder confidence) |
| Bank-facing | **דנה** (sharp, conservative) or **שירה** (formal) |
| Government grant (IIA / IIA-Yozma) | **שירה** (formal-classical) |
| Internal new-product business case | **דנה** (analytical) or **יואל** |

## Validation gates

- [ ] Executive summary captures everything
- [ ] Market sized credibly
- [ ] Financial projections internally consistent
- [ ] Funding ask explicit
- [ ] Team bios show why-this-team-can-do-this
- [ ] Persona voice held
- [ ] Hebrew grammar formal-business
