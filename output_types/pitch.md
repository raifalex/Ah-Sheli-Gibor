# Output Type: Pitch

A pitch is a structured, persuasive argument delivered in a fixed time window (5 to 20 minutes). The unit is the deck-slide, the elevator ride, or the room of decision-makers. Hebrew tech pitches in 2026 carry specific conventions.

## When this output type is right

- **Investor pitch** (Series A, B, C) — 12–15 slides
- **Customer pitch** (enterprise sales) — 8–10 slides
- **Internal pitch** (proposing a project, budget, hire) — shorter, denser
- **Elevator pitch** — 60 to 90 seconds verbal
- **Conference pitch** (Disrupt-style competition, demo day) — 4 to 8 minutes

## Structural template (default — 12-slide investor)

1. **Hook** — one sentence that names what's broken (problem-statement)
2. **Problem detail** — concrete pain, ideally quantified
3. **Solution** — what you built
4. **Market size** — TAM/SAM/SOM with credible sources
5. **Traction** — ARR, customers, growth rate, retention
6. **Why now** — what changed in the world (regulation, technology, behavior)
7. **Why us** — team credentials, unique insight
8. **Business model** — how revenue flows
9. **Competitive landscape** — 2×2 matrix or feature comparison
10. **Roadmap** — 12 / 24-month vision
11. **Ask** — round size, use of funds, who's leading
12. **Close** — single-sentence vision + contact

## Hebrew-specific conventions

- **Numbers digital throughout.** No spelled-out *שלוש-עשר אלף לקוחות*. Always *13,000 לקוחות*.
- **Approximation marker כ-** for ranges: *כ-3 מיליון בקשות בחודש*, not *בערך 3 מיליון*.
- **Acronyms preserved English** in formal pitch register: ARR, GTM, ICP, NRR, TAM, SAM. Brand names always English: AWS, Anthropic, OpenAI.
- **Smikhut for compound business terms** when both parts are Hebrew: *מנהל המוצר, ראש הצוות, מנהלת השיווק*. Hybrid OK with hyphen: *מנהל ה-product, ראש ה-revenue*.
- **No filler words** at all (אז / כאילו / בעצם removed).
- **Sentence length** — pitch sentences are 1–2 clauses. Save the long sentence for the closing vision.

## Slide-text conventions

- **Headline** — 4–8 words max, no period
- **Subheadline** — one sentence, period optional
- **Body** — 3 bullet max, each bullet 1 line
- **Footer/source** — citation in 8–10pt

## Persona pairings

| Pitch type | Best persona |
|---|---|
| Investor pitch (tech-forward, momentum-driven) | **יואל** |
| Investor pitch (values-driven, narrative arc) | **שירה** or **איתמר** |
| Enterprise sales (regulated industry, sharp soundbites) | **דנה** |
| Internal pitch (proposing a project) | **יואל** for engineering / **דנה** for cross-functional |
| Demo day / conference competition | **יואל** with **גלעד** opener |

**Slide 1 — Hook:**

>
> 73% מצוותי ה-Engineering שניסו להריץ agents בפרודקשן ב-2025 נטשו את הפרויקט.

## Validation gates (pre-output)

Before outputting a pitch, verify:

- [ ] Every number is digital (not spelled-out)
- [ ] Every percentage has the % symbol, no Hebrew word "אחוז"
- [ ] Every English acronym preserved English with hyphen-prefix where needed (ב-ARR, ה-GTM)
- [ ] No filler words in any slide
- [ ] No anti-patterns from `references/anti_patterns.md`
- [ ] Persona consistency: every slide reads as the chosen persona
- [ ] Story arc: hook → problem → solution → proof → close
