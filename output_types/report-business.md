# Output Type: Business Report

Quarterly business report, market analysis, internal performance review, or annual summary. Longer + more detailed than executive memo; less recommendation-driven, more comprehensive.

## When this output type is right

- **Quarterly business review (QBR)** — internal review of department/company performance
- **Annual report** (private; public companies use Israeli Securities Authority format)
- **Market analysis report** — sizing, competitive landscape, recommendations
- **Strategic review** — annual / mid-year strategy retrospective
- **Investor letter** (Buffett-style) — narrative + quantitative

## Length

5–25 pages typical. QBR: 8–15. Market analysis: 15–25. Annual report: 20–40 (private).

## Structural template

### Cover + executive summary (~1 page)
TL;DR — main findings, metrics highlights, 3–5 key conclusions.

### Performance metrics (~2–5 pages)
- Headline numbers (revenue, growth, retention, etc.)
- YoY / QoQ comparison
- Vs. plan / vs. forecast
- Cohort analysis if relevant
- Customer / market segments

### Analysis (~2–8 pages)
- What drove the numbers
- What worked / what didn't
- External factors (market, regulatory, macro)
- Customer behavior shifts

### Strategic implications (~1–3 pages)
- What this means for next quarter / year
- Strategic options considered
- Recommended path
- Resource implications

### Risks & opportunities (~1–2 pages)
- Top 5 risks + mitigations
- Top 5 opportunities + investment needed

### Appendices
- Detailed financial tables
- Customer cohorts
- Source data

## Hebrew-specific conventions

- **Numbers digital** with full currency markers
- **Charts and tables** labeled in Hebrew + English (bilingual leadership)
- **YoY / QoQ** preserved English (Hebrew alternatives feel stilted)
- **Smikhut for compound business terms** (סך הכל הכנסות / שיעור צמיחה / רווח גולמי)
- **No filler words**

## Persona pairings

| Report type | Best persona |
|---|---|
| Internal QBR | **דנה** (sharp analysis) or **יואל** (founder voice) |
| External annual report | **שירה** (formal-classical) or **איתמר** (patient authority) |
| Market analysis | **איתמר** (analytical depth) |
| Investor letter | **שירה** (literary) or **יואל** (founder mission) |

## Validation gates

- [ ] Executive summary holds the full story
- [ ] All numbers cited consistently across body and appendices
- [ ] YoY / QoQ comparison present for every headline metric
- [ ] Analysis explains causation, not just description
- [ ] Strategic implications connect to numbers
- [ ] Risks specific (not generic)
- [ ] Persona voice consistent throughout
- [ ] Hebrew grammar clean

## Related output types

- For shorter decision-grade brief: `report-executive`
- For technical / policy depth: `report-whitepaper`
- For external one-pager: `case-study`
