# Output Type: Case Study

Customer success story / case study. Narrative + quantitative. Used by B2B sales teams, content marketing, conference talks.

## When this output type is right

- **B2B customer case study** — for sales / marketing
- **Conference talk case study** — short version of customer story
- **Investor case study** — proving product-market fit with named customer
- **Industry analyst case study** — for Gartner / Forrester / IDC inclusion
- **Award submission case study** — industry award narrative

## Length

500–3,000 words. Marketing case study: 800–1,500. Long-form: 2,000–3,000.

## Structural template

### Headline
Quantified result in 5–9 words. "How [Customer] achieved [Result] with [Solution]."

### Customer profile (~150 words)
- Industry + company size
- Hebrew + English names
- Geography
- Use case briefly

### The challenge (~300 words)
- What problem they faced
- Why existing solutions failed
- Stakes of not solving

### The approach (~400 words)
- How they evaluated solutions
- Why they chose this one
- Implementation overview

### The implementation (~400 words)
- Timeline (weeks 1-4, 5-8, etc.)
- Team involved
- Challenges + how addressed

### The results (~400 words)
- Quantified outcomes (% improvement, $ saved, hours reduced)
- Customer quote (direct, named, attributed)
- Before/after comparison

### What's next (~150 words)
- Expansion plans
- Lessons learned
- Quote on future

## Hebrew-specific conventions

- **Quantified metrics** prominently — Israeli buyers value specifics
- **Customer names** in original (Hebrew for Israeli, English for international)
- **Founder / leader quotes** direct — preserve their voice
- **Numbers digital** with currency / time units
- **No hyperbole** — Israelis dismiss puffery; under-promise + over-deliver in tone
- **Smikhut for compound terms**

## Persona pairings

- **דנה** (sharp positioning) or **יואל** (mission framing) or **איתמר** (depth)

## Validation gates

- [ ] Quantified result in headline
- [ ] At least 3 specific numbers in body
- [ ] At least 1 named direct customer quote
- [ ] Implementation timeline explicit
- [ ] Hebrew grammar clean
- [ ] No hyperbole ("revolutionary" / "game-changing" without evidence)
- [ ] Persona voice held
